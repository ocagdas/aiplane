# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, identities and levels of experience. Treat people respectfully, explain technical disagreements with evidence, accept constructive feedback, and help newcomers participate.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, give warnings, restrict participation or remove participants for serious or repeated violations. Decisions should be proportionate, explain the conduct involved, and protect affected people. Maintainers are subject to the same standards and should recuse themselves from reviewing reports about their own conduct where another maintainer is available.

Report sensitive incidents privately through a contact channel listed by the repository maintainer or the private collaboration channel through which access was arranged. Do not publish private incident details in an issue. If no private route is listed, request a reporting route without identifying affected people or describing sensitive details. Immediate threats can also be reported through the hosting platform's abuse-reporting process. This project is volunteer-maintained and cannot promise emergency support.
